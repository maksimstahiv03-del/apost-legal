APOST Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Policy explains how Maksym Stakhiv (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the APOST macOS desktop application. APOST lets one user connect one TikTok account and prepare, schedule, or publish videos through TikTok's official APIs. TikTok is an independent service and handles information under its own policies.

1. Information handled by APOST

  • TikTok connection: TikTok authorization and access tokens, refresh token if issued, permissions, connection status, and the TikTok account's open ID and display name returned by TikTok.
  • Video library and posts: video file paths and names, optional copies and thumbnails, technical video metadata, captions and hashtags you enter, publishing settings, scheduled times, and post status or error history.
  • App settings and diagnostics: settings you choose and local diagnostic logs. Logs may include technical error details and video names. APOST does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services in this version.
  • Support messages: information you choose to send to the contact email below.

2. How information is stored

APOST does not operate a cloud account database. App settings, the local video library, schedules, post history, and logs are stored on your Mac in the APOST application support folder. Video files remain at the locations you select unless you enable APOST's option to make a local copy. The TikTok client key and app settings are stored in the APOST application support folder. The TikTok client secret and account tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. The app sends information to TikTok only as needed for authorization, account information, video upload, publishing, and status checks that you request.

3. How information is used

APOST uses this information to connect your TikTok account, show your account name and permissions, manage your local video library and schedule, send the video and publishing details to TikTok when you request a post, check publication status, display errors, and maintain the application. We do not use app data for advertising or sell it.

4. TikTok and website hosting

When you connect or publish, TikTok receives authorization requests and the account, video, caption, settings, and status information required by the TikTok API feature you use. TikTok independently determines how it processes and retains that information. Review TikTok's privacy terms for details. TikTok may process information in countries other than yours.

These public Terms and Privacy Policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit these pages, GitHub may receive routine technical request information under its own privacy policy. APOST does not send your local library, schedules, tokens, or videos to GitHub.

5. Security

APOST stores TikTok tokens in the macOS Keychain and local app records in files on your Mac. TikTok API requests use HTTPS. Protect your Mac with its account and device security features. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Local videos, metadata, schedules, settings, and history remain on your Mac until you remove them. Use APOST's controls to delete items or disconnect TikTok. Disconnecting attempts to revoke the TikTok token and removes APOST's local token record. Directly revoking access in TikTok may stop the token working but does not necessarily erase APOST's local user-token record; use APOST's disconnect control as well. Disconnect does not delete the separate TikTok client secret. To remove it, delete the APOST-TikTok client_secret item from macOS Keychain Access. To remove remaining local APOST records and logs, quit the app and delete its application support folder. Original video files are not deleted by removing APOST's database unless you separately delete them. Diagnostic logs rotate locally, retaining the current log and up to three rotated files. TikTok retains information it receives according to its own policies.

7. Sharing

APOST does not sell or share app data for advertising. Information leaves the Mac when you use TikTok features, as described above, or when you choose to email us. macOS provides local Keychain and notification functions. Other than these services, APOST in this version has no cloud storage, analytics, or crash-reporting provider.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

You choose whether to connect TikTok and which content to add, schedule, or publish. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by the operator by emailing us. Rights and response timeframes depend on the laws that apply to you. Since APOST's library and schedules are stored locally, you can manage or delete those records directly on your Mac. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator.

9. Children

APOST is not designed for children. Use the application only if you meet the age and eligibility rules that apply to you and your TikTok account. We do not knowingly collect children's information through APOST.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy if APOST or its data practices change. We will revise the effective date above and provide notice of material changes where required by law.
