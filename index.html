APOST

APOST is a macOS desktop app that lets one user connect one TikTok account and schedule or publish videos using TikTok's official APIs.

You control which TikTok account to connect and which videos to schedule or publish. TikTok authorization and platform features remain subject to TikTok's own terms and policies.

APOST is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
